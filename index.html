Process

How it
runs.

One point of contact from the first call to launch day.

  1. Call

    Thirty minutes on what you sell, who buys it, and the sites you wish were yours.

  2. Direction

    One visual direction with the first screen and the hero image, for a clear yes or no.

  3. Build

    Every page designed, written and built. You review on a private link.

  4. Launch

    Live on your domain, with the launch film and the first posts ready.

Questions

Good to
know.

Where do the images come from?

Your own photos when you have them. When you don't, I create photoreal images for your brand with AI tools, so the site never relies on stock photos everyone has seen.

Do you write the text?

Yes. Copy is part of every package, written from a short call and anything you already have.

Can I edit the site myself afterwards?

Yes. Text, images and prices can be changed without touching code, and I show you how at handover.

What do you need from me?

Thirty minutes for the first call, your logo if you have one, and quick answers when I send the direction.

Are the sites shown real clients?

They are concept projects for fictional brands, made to show the range of what I build. Each one is a live, working website.
